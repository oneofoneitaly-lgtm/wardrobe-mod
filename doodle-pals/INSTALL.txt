DOODLE PALS  -  Minecraft 1.21.1 (Forge)   by @Adryh01
======================================================

INSTALL
  Install Forge 52.1.20 (or newer 52.x) and copy doodle-pals-1.21.1-*.jar into "mods".
  On a server, the server needs the jar too.

PLAY
  Craft the Sketchbook: book + ink sac + feather (also in the creative menu).
  Right click it, press J, or type /doodle to start drawing.
  Bringing a drawing to life costs 1 Ink Sac (free in creative). Up to 3 pals at once.

  Right click your pal ............ wait / follow
  Shift + right click ............. store it as a Doodle Page (use the page to wake it again)
  Right click with ink ............ heal it
  /doodle call .................... bring your pals back to you
  A pal that dies drops its page, so you never lose a drawing.

COLOURS AND POWERS  (a colour counts when it covers at least 12% of the drawing; up to 3 powers)
  Red Fire      hits burn, cannot burn        Orange Brave   hits harder
  Yellow Spark  your speed                    Green Nature   grows crops
  Cyan Frost    slows and freezes             Blue Tide      underwater breathing and swimming
  Purple Magic  ranged magic bolts            Pink Heart     heals you when badly hurt
  White Air     slow falling                  Black Shadow   sneak to vanish, weakens enemies
  Brown Rock    your resistance, more health  Gray Iron      your mining speed, pal armour
  More colour = stronger power. Bigger drawings are tougher but slower.
