MYTHORIA  -  fantasy modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Forge)   by @Adryh01
=======================================================================

INSTALL
  1. Install Forge 52.1.20 (or newer 52.x) for Minecraft 1.21.1.
  2. Copy the five mythoria-*.jar files into your "mods" folder.
     (Or import the CurseForge .zip / .mrpack in your launcher.)
  On a server, put the same five jars on the server too.

PLAY
  M  or  /market ............ open the Market
  Crowns .................... defeat monsters and mine ores (stone sometimes too)
  /crowns ................... show your wallet
  /crowns give <player> <n> . operators only

CREATURES
  Golden Griffin   wild in plains, meadows and savannas. Tame it with meat or buy the egg.
  Ember Drake      wild (rare) in badlands, deserts and stony peaks. Tame with meat or buy the egg.
                   While riding, R breathes fire (change the key in Options > Controls).
  Mounts           Right click: ride. Space: climb. Look down + W: dive. Shift: dismount.
                   Shift + right click: wait / follow.
  Spirit Fox       wild (rare) in taiga and snowy lands. Tame with sweet berries or a Fairy Cake,
                   or buy the charm. It fetches drops and lets you see in the dark.
  Crystal Golem    boss. Rare in dark places, or summoned with the Golem Core.
  Shadow Lich      boss. Summoned with the Lich Phylactery.

WEAPONS     Phoenix Blade, Frost Fang, Storm Blade, Void Reaver  (right click uses their power)
            Elven Bow (full draw: three arrows), Arcane Staff, Life Staff
ARMOR       Dragon  - full set: fire immunity
            Elven   - full set: speed and high jumps
            Frost   - full set: no freezing, breathe under water
FEAST       Moon Apple, Fairy Cake, Dragon Drumstick, Elven Mead,
            Star Fruit, Frost Sorbet, Honey Pie, Mana Potion
