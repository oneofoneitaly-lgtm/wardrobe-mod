Inventory Styles - by @Adryh01
Minecraft 1.21.1 with Forge 52

1. Install Forge for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Put inventory-styles-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar in your "mods" folder.
3. Start the game, then press I (or click "Style" inside any inventory).

Only you need it: it also works on servers without the mod.
