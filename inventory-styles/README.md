# Inventory Styles — by @Adryh01

A Forge 1.21.1 mod that changes only one thing: the look of your inventories.

## Styles
| Style | Look |
|---|---|
| Dark Glass | See-through dark panels with a coloured rim |
| Fantasy | Parchment, wooden frames and golden corners |
| Neon | Black with glowing lines |
| Pastel | Soft light colours with rounded slots |
| Vanilla | The normal Minecraft look |

## What gets restyled
Inventory (and recipe book), Creative, Chests (also barrel, shulker box, dispenser, hopper),
Crafting (crafting table, crafter, stonecutter, loom, cartography table), Furnaces (furnace,
blast furnace, smoker), Magic (enchanting, brewing, beacon), Smithing (anvil, smithing table,
grindstone), Trading (villagers, horse) and the Hotbar.

Every group can have its own style. The colour of Dark Glass, Neon and Pastel can be changed
(violet, cyan, emerald, gold, rose, red).

## How to use
* Press **I**, or click the **Style** button in the corner of any inventory.
* Or type `/invstyle`, `/invstyle set chests neon`, `/invstyle set all pastel`, `/invstyle colour cyan`.
* Changes are instant and saved in `config/invstyles.properties`.

Client only: it works on any server. Screens added by other mods keep their own look.
